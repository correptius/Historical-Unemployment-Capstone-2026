# Historical-Unemployment-Capstone-2026

### Data science capstone project for the Montgomery College 2026 spring semester in DATA205. 

By Ria Rhodes

## Overview:

This project is a historical story analysis of unemployment rates, rate changes, and rate causes. It delves in to unemployment statistics within Arkansas, Arizona, California, Maine, New Jersey, and Tennessee. The final goal of this project is to determine causes of unemployment rates over spans of time, allowing us to understand why the rate of unemployment has been altered. It looks at states across the U.S. to gain a wide span of knowledge, with each state having diverse social and phsyical layouts to create a potential for notable difference.
